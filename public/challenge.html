How it worksThree rounds

A challenge is not a comment. It is a position, against a named opponent, on a market that will resolve — which is why it can be scored and why it goes on both records.

Round 01
Pick their call, take the other side

Every opponent below is holding a real, still-tradeable position. Their side is already chosen — yours is whatever is left. If they are on YES, you are on NO.

Round 02
Stake FLEX, write the thesis

Up to 100 FLEX, escrowed the moment you send. Declined or ignored, you get it back. A stake needs a real market attached — otherwise nothing could ever settle it.

Round 03
The market settles it

No voting, no judges. When the market resolves, the winner is decided automatically and the result lands on both public records — including yours if you lose.
