How the numbers are built

Pool /day, band (max qualifying spread from mid), and min order size come straight from Polymarket's reward config on each market (Gamma clobRewards). In-band depth is the live resting liquidity inside the reward band on both sides of the book, read from the CLOB order book right now — it tells you how crowded the pool already is.

Estimated yield is a first-order model, not a guarantee: daily_pool × 100 / (in_band_depth + 100) — your $100 earning a pro-rata share of the pool against the liquidity already sitting in the band. Polymarket's exact reward-scoring function (two-sided Qmin weighting, spread-proximity scoring, in-game multipliers) isn't fully published, so treat this as a ranking signal, not a promised payout. Where a book can't be read, depth and yield show — rather than a fabricated number.
